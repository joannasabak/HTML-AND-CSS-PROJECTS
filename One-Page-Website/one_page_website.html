Short guide to hikes in Picos de Europa

The Picos de Europa are a striking limestone mountain range in northern Spain,
rising just 15 kilometers (9 miles) from the Cantabrian Sea.

Geography and Landscape

Location: They stretch across Asturias, Cantabria, and Castile and León, forming part of the Cantabrian Mountains.
The Name: Sailors returning from the Americas named them the "Peaks of Europe" because these towering jagged walls were the first visible sight of the continent.
Three Massifs: Rivers carve the range into three distinct sections:
Western Massif (Cornión): Includes the famous glacial Lakes of Covadonga.Central Massif (Urrieles):
Contains the highest peaks, including the iconic Picu Urriellu (Naranjo de Bulnes) and the highest summit overall, Torre de Cerredo (2,650 meters / 8,690 feet).
Eastern Massif (Ándara): The lowest and most compact of the three.

How to get there?

The easiest way is to fly into Santander Airport, Asturias Airport, or Bilbao Airport, and then rent a car or take a regional bus.

Top Day Hikes

  • Ruta del Cares (Poncebos to Caín):


    Difficulty: Moderate
    Distance: ~21–24 km (out-and-back)
    Details: Follows a breathtaking path cut into the cliffs alongside the Cares River. It is relatively flat but features steep drop-offs and narrow stretches.

  • Lakes of Covadonga & Mirador de Ordiales:


    Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
    Distance: 6 to 10 km
    Details: A gentle loop past glacial lakes (Enol and Ercina) where cows graze freely. You can extend the route upward to the Ordiales Scenic Balcony for panoramic views over the valleys.

  • Fuente Dé to Áliva Valley:


    Difficulty: Easy / Low-effort, high-reward
    Distance: Varies (~14 km if walking down)
    Details: Ride the Fuente Dé Cable Car up to high-altitude crags, then enjoy a scenic, steady descent back down through green pastures and the Áliva valley.

  • Refugio Vega de Urriellu

    (from Pandébano):
    Difficulty: High / Challenging
    Distance: ~13 km round trip
    Details: Ascends to the base of the iconic Naranjo de Bulnes (Picu Urriellu) tower, a legendary spot for climbers and high-altitude trekkers.
